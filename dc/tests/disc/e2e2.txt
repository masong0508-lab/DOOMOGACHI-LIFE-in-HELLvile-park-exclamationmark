# e2e2.txt - DC9C on Flycast: the restart. Same VMU image as e2e1.txt, a new power-on:
# the title knows the save (its Continue row), loads it, and the island is the
# one that was saved: loaded again at once, its town crc is the one e2e1.txt
# printed (@CRC@ for run_flycast.sh). The game plays on after the Continue (rent
# comes in, time passes), so the crc is taken on the load command, not on the
# running island.
wait 150
expect-scene TITLE
expect-log story -, free play saved
expect-save 1
press START
wait 24
shot e2e_title_menu
press A
wait 400
expect-log DC: vmu read DOOMO1.SYS
expect-scene ISLAND
shot e2e_loaded
load 1
expect-town-crc @CRC@
