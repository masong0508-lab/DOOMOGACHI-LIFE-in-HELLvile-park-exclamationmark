# hellvile.txt - DC7: the busiest overworld hub on the Dreamcast. A free
# stroll in Hellvile (scene STORY 11: the residents, props and lamps of the
# bad side of the park), walking a loop of about 12 s, by day and at night;
# a shot at each corner of the loop. The 30 Hz check (mark-ticks /
# expect-ticks) runs over the whole walk in Dreamcast frames, and dcrun
# prints the game's work per tick and the 3D view's build time (mailbox
# g3d_*) at the end. Native runner lines only, so the same script plays on
# `runner` for the reference shots.
scene STORY 11
wait 60
expect-scene STORY
shot day_start
hold RIGHT 45
shot day_east
hold DOWN 30
shot day_south
hold LEFT 60
shot day_west
hold UP 30
shot day_back
hold RIGHT 15
wait 10
shot day_end
