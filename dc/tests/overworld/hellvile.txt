# hellvile.txt - DC7: the busiest overworld hub on the Dreamcast. A free
# stroll in Hellvile (scene STORY 11: the residents, props and lamps of the
# bad side of the park), walking a loop of about 12 s, by day and at night;
# a shot at each corner of the loop. dcrun prints the game's work per tick
# and the 3D view's build time (mailbox g3d_*) at the end, and
# run_flycast.sh fails the walk if any tick's work passes 33.3 ms (the 30 Hz
# budget). Native runner lines only (no mark-ticks / frames, which the
# native runner lacks), so the same script plays on `runner` for the
# reference shots and in the host runner.
scene STORY 11
wait 60
expect-scene STORY
shot day_start
hold RIGHT 45
shot day_east
hold DOWN 30
shot day_south
hold LEFT 60
shot day_west
hold UP 30
shot day_back
hold RIGHT 15
wait 10
shot day_end
