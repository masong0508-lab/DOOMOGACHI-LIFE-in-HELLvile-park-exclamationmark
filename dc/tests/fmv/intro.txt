# intro.txt - DC9C on Flycast: the boot FMV (INTRO.DMV on the disc) plays, then the
# game boots to the title. Run with --fmv (the harness leaves the movie off
# otherwise): dcrun ... --fmv --fresh --script dc/tests/fmv/intro.txt
# `frames` counts 60 Hz video frames and works while the game's own tick counter
# is still 0. The movie starts about 3 s in (the BIOS' own screen, the disc,
# the VMU) and runs 10.1 s (152 frames at 15 fps); the shots land in its blue
# opening, its dark middle and on the ident's name.
frames 330
shot fmv_a
frames 240
shot fmv_b
frames 210
shot fmv_c
wait-log 600 DC: fmv: done
print-frames fmv_done
expect-log DC: fmv: /cd/INTRO.DMV, 152 frames
expect-log 152 frames shown, 0 skipped
wait-log 60 doomo: init
wait 160
expect-scene TITLE
print-frames title_after_fmv
shot fmv_title
