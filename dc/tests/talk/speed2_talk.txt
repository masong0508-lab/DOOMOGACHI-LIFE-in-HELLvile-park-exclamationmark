# speed2_talk.txt - TD3: the Talk screen's frame times on the Dreamcast (sh dc/harness/speed2.sh OUT talk; make -C dc talk-speed).
# The GBA harness format of the other speed2 groups, so tools/speed2.py turns it into a dcrun script; only
# the Dreamcast plays it (Talk is not built on the PS1, and the GBA's Talk numbers are in docs/TALK.md 6j).
# Every window holds waits and plain presses only, so its ticks include what a player's press causes: the
# answer made one generator step a tick (TD2) and typed out. The talk-* lines between windows only move
# the screen to the next place (they are not measured).
wait 40
expect-log doomo: init
sync on 61
seed 18
scene TITLE
wait 10
# the bank comes off the disc in slices after boot (talkdisc.c): not a window, the wait is the disc's
talk-ready
push HOME 3
wait 20
press B
wait 20
push HOME 3
wait 60
talk-enter Nana
fps start
wait 60
fps talk: opener, greeting box typing
shot talk_opener
talk-read
fps start
wait 90
fps talk: list at rest
shot talk_list
press DOWN
wait 2
fps start
wait 30
fps talk: list cursor move
press UP
wait 2
fps start
press A
wait 150
fps talk: quick reply made and typed
shot talk_quick
talk-read
press SELECT
wait 4
fps start
press A
wait 30
fps talk: category page open
shot talk_category
fps start
press A
wait 150
fps talk: act said, answer made and typed
shot talk_act
talk-read
talk-page more
fps start
wait 60
fps talk: Options page
shot talk_options
talk-page main
talk-leave
fps start
wait 90
fps talk: back in the house after goodbye
