# TD2: the typed line. talk-type feeds the characters to the text entry the way the native runner does;
# talk-key types the same words through the Maple keyboard on port 2 (dcrun --device 2 keyboard: the
# game's pad.c turns each key into a character for ui_text_entry_feed). In the native runner talk-key
# is played as talk-type (make -C dc talk-test does that), so both give the same words and the same pictures.
scene TITLE
# the Talk bank comes off the disc in slices after boot: wait for it on the title screen
talk-ready
wait 10
push HOME 3
wait 4
press B
wait 10
talk-enter Nana
talk-read
wait 4
shot typed_0_ready
# the line through the keyboard: the row opens the entry, the keys type, Enter sends
talk-key "i love the pie"
expect-talk-you "pie"
wait 90
shot typed_1_said
talk-read
shot typed_2_answer
# a second line, a question, with a capital letter and punctuation (Shift + key)
talk-key "Nana, what is in the pie?"
expect-talk-you "pie"
wait 90
shot typed_3_question
talk-read
# a line the safety gate refuses: Talk says so and goes on (the same gate as everywhere)
talk-key "let us have sex"
expect-talk-gate refuse
wait 90
shot typed_4_refused
talk-read
talk-leave
expect-talk off
