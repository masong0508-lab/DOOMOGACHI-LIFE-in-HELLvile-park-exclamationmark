# TD1 (Talk on the Dreamcast): Talk's database comes from the disc image.
# assert-no-stubs
# The CDI carries TALK_EN.BIN (dc/tools/mkcdi.sh); talk-bank open reads it whole through /cd (dc/src/talkdisc.c),
# opens it with talk_db_open and verifies every bank's CRC over the heap copy; line makes one greeting with the
# generator from the first resident (it goes to the log); enter opens the real Talk screen the way the house
# does (home_talk_enter), which draws the resident's opener. Run on the CDI (an ELF boot has no disc: the bank
# is "not here" and Talk stays off, as before TD1):
#   dc/harness/build/dcrun /opt/toolchains/dc/flycast_libretro.so dc/build/doomogachi.cdi --nm dc/build/doomogachi.nm \
#       --fresh --native-seed 0x5EED1234 --script dc/tests/talk/talk_disc.txt --shots OUT
scene TITLE
wait 10
# the first knock moves the premade island in (an empty runner town), as home_visit does
push HOME 3
wait 4
press B
wait 10
talk-bank open
talk-bank line
talk-bank enter
wait 30
# the resident's opener is a dialogue box over the Talk screen: the top scene is DIALOG until it is read
expect-scene DIALOG
shot talk_disc_opener
press A
wait 30
press A
wait 30
press A
wait 30
shot talk_disc_ready
