# TD2: Talk on the Dreamcast with the pad alone (no keyboard on any port): the opener, a quick reply (one
# press), catch-the-word (the quick reply that steers to the topic on offer), a topic from Options, a
# compliment, the "Type your own line..." row (it opens the on-screen keyboard: the pad types there, the
# keyboard is optional), goodbye. The same commands play in the native runner (core/native/runner_main.c),
# so make -C dc talk-test compares every shot with the native 320x240 picture.
scene TITLE
# the Talk bank comes off the disc in slices after boot: wait for it on the title screen
talk-ready
wait 10
push HOME 3
wait 4
press B
wait 10
talk-enter Nana
wait 20
shot pad_0_opener
talk-read
expect-talk on
expect-talk-page main
expect-talk-row "Type your own line..."
# The resident's original little oddities must also be selectable with a Dreamcast pad.
expect-talk-row "Tell me one little oddity"
wait 4
shot pad_1_ready
# a quick reply: the top row, one press
talk-quick 0
wait 90
shot pad_2_quick_said
talk-read
# catch-the-word: the third quick reply picks up the word the resident just offered ("Random, but... music?")
talk-quick 2
wait 90
shot pad_3_catch
talk-read
shot pad_4_caught
# a kind word from the category page
talk-say compliment
expect-talk-you "You're great"
wait 90
shot pad_5_compliment
talk-read
# a topic act: Ask > (no topic picked: "Tell me about <the topic on offer>")
talk-say ask_about
expect-talk-you "Tell me about"
talk-read
# Options, then the typing row: the on-screen keyboard opens for the pad
talk-page more
expect-talk-page more
expect-talk-row "Topic:"
wait 4
shot pad_6_options
talk-page main
talk-leave
expect-talk off
wait 90
shot pad_7_after
