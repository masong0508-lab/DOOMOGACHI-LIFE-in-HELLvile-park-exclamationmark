- **PLAY-30 (2026-11-17, tried and reverted; measured on the GBA):** the PLAY tab's pad bodies (round rect + shadow line, ~6 rect calls a pad, 8 pads) were baked into pictures (`e43eca1`) to take the recording loop under 33.3 ms. **It was a regression.** GBA PLAY, before (step 3, `0229f90`) vs after PLAY-30 (`e43eca1`): idle avg / max 23.0 / 25.8 ms vs 27.0 / 29.8 ms; looping avg / max 24.4 / 31.5 ms vs 28.4 / 33.9 ms; `vid_blit` share of the profile 3.9% vs 21.6%. It cost about 4 ms a frame and pushed the loop peak past the budget. The pads are back to direct rect fills (`pad_cell` in `studio_play.c`; no `st_bake`, key kind 0x34 is free again). **Lesson:** do not bake large, mostly solid shapes. A ~1,300 px blit costs far more on the GBA than the handful of rect fills it replaces; bake a shape only when it is many small rects (the SONG record dial, the LYRICS strip) and measure on the GBA before landing. The "33.4 ms" loop peak in the ST-PLAY note was stale: before PLAY-30 the loop peak was already 31.5 ms, under the budget, so there was nothing to fix. Host tests and byte-identical screenshots cannot show this kind of loss: only a GBA run can.
