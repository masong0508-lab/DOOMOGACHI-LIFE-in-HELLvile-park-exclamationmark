# Grumbleguts: romance option (adults only)

Grumbleguts is now a real resident of Hellvile (roster id 729, `ROSTER_ID_GRUMBLEGUTS`,
replacing the old gate guard Bunkerton, whose two friendships he inherited). Because he is a
resident, he plugs into the normal life sim: any adult resident can fall for him, confess,
date him, propose and marry him, and he can fall for them. `romance=ANY`, he/him, birthday
Feb 14, night owl, `social=99`.

* Adults only, enforced by the sim: `sim_romance_ok` already refuses kids outright and refuses
  teens with non-teens. Grumbleguts has no KID or TEEN trait, so he can only ever pair with adults.
* Consent and comedy: confessions can be rejected like anyone else's (running gag: his only
  crush is Gatekeep, the Hillvile robot who has never opened the gate and is not interested).
* Exes: Shriekra and Spearmint (EX edges), for cutscene and news-ticker cameos.
* Tone: the dating and wedding news lines are cheeky and closed-door ("Gate's shut. Don't knock.",
  "Suite closed till Monday."). Nothing explicit is shown; the joke is the implication.
