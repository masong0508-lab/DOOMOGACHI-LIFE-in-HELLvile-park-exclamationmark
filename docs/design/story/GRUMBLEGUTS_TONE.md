# Grumbleguts tone notes (supersede the older wholesome framing in GRUMBLEGUTS_AND_DIRECTOR_NOTES.md)

- PERSONALITY REWORK (user, supersedes the wholesome/"good-natured, affectionate" tone above): Grumbleguts is NOT a cuddly glutton. He's a sensual, shamelessly promiscuous, smooth-talking devil in the Red Guy / Angel Dust mould: slow drawl, bedroom eyes, pet names (darlin', hon, sugar), compliments that are really propositions, lingering looks, a purr in the voice, and the belly/butt as deliberate come-ons. Edgy, sarcastic, a bit of a bad influence. BUT he has a real friendly side when it counts: he is a loyal EDGY ANTIHERO who drops the act to save his friends (steps in front of danger, holds the gate shut for them, hauls the player out of the Underbelly, grumbles that he "totally didn't do that for you"). Sweet moments always get undercut with a smirk. Keep the same hard BOUNDARIES: innuendo only, adults only, consensual, cartoon-modest, kids/teens never targeted, rejections still a running gag. Lines already changed in core/src/levels.c (boss subtitle, intro taunt, defeat toast, level flavour) and the Feast opening panel in core/src/cutscene.c; any new dialogue/bestiary text should follow this voice.

- ADULT HUMOR PASS (user: "this is not a kids game", wants SUPER EDGY adult humor; supersedes any kid-friendly softening): Grumbleguts is written for adults. Dirty-minded double entendres, dark and gallows humor, hell puns, swearing in the PG-13-to-R range (damn, hell, ass, bastard, the odd f-bomb on BRUTAL), shameless sleaze, and punchlines that land on the straight man. He is crude on purpose and proud of it. Keep it implication and innuendo: the joke is the setup, never the act.

No explicit sex acts, no genitals, no nudity beyond cartoon cheeks, everything consensual and played for laughs.
