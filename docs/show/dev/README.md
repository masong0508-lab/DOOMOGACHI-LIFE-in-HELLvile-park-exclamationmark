# Show development material (superseded)

This folder keeps the pitch round that produced the pilot, so the decisions in the show documents can be traced back to it. **None of it is a working document.** Do not board, record, cast or pitch from anything here; the documents one level up win every conflict, and `../PILOT_ROADMAP.md` is the way in.

| File | What it is | What replaced it |
|---|---|---|
| `pitch_A.md` | Pitch A, "Arrival": the faithful Chapter 1 arrival pilot | Its quiet material became Eps 102-104 (`../SERIES_OVERVIEW.md` §5.3) |
| `pitch_B.md` | Pitch B, "Two Sides": a 2x11 pilot with a Hellvile half | Its Hellvile material became the 2x11 episodes from 108; its population runner and wall grammar were grafted into 101 |
| `pitch_C.md` | Pitch C, "Mingle Night": the winning pitch, before the judges' grafts | `../PILOT_TREATMENT.md` and `../PILOT_BEATSHEET.md`; every change is in the graft ledger, `../PRODUCTION_ROADMAP.md` §1.3 |
| `digest_canon.md` | A reading digest of the repo canon, made for the pitch round | `docs/design/story/story_final.md` is the canon and wins every conflict |
| `digest_assets.md` | An inventory of the repo's reusable art, toolkit and audio | `../PRODUCTION_ROADMAP.md` §4 |

**Known gaps against the standards now in force.** The pitches were written before the standards pass and are kept as written, with a banner at the top of each. Among other things they contain 1-frame and 3-frame inserts (the show's minimum is 4 frames), a 1-frame LCD pentagram (no pentagram imagery in Season 1), the trailer's Pump Shotgun (now the Pump Sprinkler), Clinker's "...She?" (cut to protect "The Bottle"), Sunny as "she" (Sunny is they), and in Pitch B a Grumbleguts pilot appearance with a flirt line through the brick where Sunny is standing (in the show he is sound only in 101, and Sunny is never his target or in his eyeline).
