## Board notes

**Composition locks** (the cold open sets up grammar the whole series uses):
* Shots 2 and 9 are the same frame. The bandstand is centred on x = 192, and in shot 9 the Wall runs exactly down that line. Every brick wipe and split screen in the series runs along it, starting with the main title's pre-chorus and the Act Four firework.
* Shots 11 and 12 share the banner's letter baseline and scale, so the match cut from crayon to pixel art lands on the same word in the same place.
* Shots 16 and 18 are the same frame, as the whiplash rule requires; the balloon's turn is the only change.
* Shot 7 is the source frame for the 4-f insert in shot 17. The hand pose and the loop must match exactly.

**Key poses to board first:** the cloud's heart eyes (6), the empty hand (7), the Wall frame (9), the Mayor's stop on the note (12), the third bloom with its drip (15), the freeze (16), the balloon facing the Wall (18), Mascotty in the plaza (19), the egg in the heart iris (21).

**Continuity:** the cupcake balloon from shot 15 is still over the N, facing front, when Sunny walks under the banner in beat 1-03. The lamp from shot 20 is echoed in Ep 102 (a lamp by the pet's bed). The music box's muted note (shot 8) is the note the Mayor stops on (shot 12) and the note he gets past in beat 4-08.

**Standards:** no faces on the young cherub or the young imp (friendship-only flashback material, held for F4); one 4-f insert, and it is a memory, not a scare; no OMINOUS cue anywhere in the cold open, so the fog at 21:00 stays the series' first real scare; firework blooms ramp over 8 f or more; no flash frames.
