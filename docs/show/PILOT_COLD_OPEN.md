**Notes for the reader.** The first eleven shots are a children's crayon storybook with captions (SUPER) in a child's hand and no narrator, so the first spoken words of the series are the Mayor's "...Hee. Hee." Sound cues in capitals (PLUCK, THUD, CRACK) are scored to the frame in the shot list. The young cherub and the young imp are never shown from the front.

### Page 1 of 4

```text
FADE IN:

INSERT - A CALENDAR PAGE - CRAYON STORYBOOK

Everything is drawn in children's-book crayon on paper
grain. The lines boil gently, the way a child's drawing
would if it could breathe.

A crayon circles one square: MIDSUMMER. Then it scribbles
fireworks all round it, happily, outside the lines.

A MUSIC BOX begins a sweet little tune in F. This is the
MINGLE.

EXT. HELLVILE PARK - BANDSTAND - NIGHT (STORYBOOK, LONG AGO)

A circle iris opens on ONE park. No Wall. A river with a
bridge. At the centre, a round bandstand strung with
lanterns.

Two long tables: a tower of cupcakes on the north side, a
cauldron of soup on the south.

SUPER, in a child's handwriting, typing on: "Every Midsummer
night, the whole park had one party."

Kids run across the bridge both ways, little bunnies one way
and little imps the other. Nobody minds.

SUPER: "It was called the Mingle."

From behind: a SMALL CHERUB, all wings and halo, holds up a
string. On the end of it bobs a small CLOUD with two dot
eyes and a smile. It blinks.

At the soup table, two small horned hands set down two
bowls. Clink. Clink.

Fireworks bloom over the bandstand, crayon-bright, on the
beat. The cloud rises to look. Its eyes turn into hearts.

SUPER: "It loved fireworks."

CLOSE ON the small hand and the loop of string. One more
firework -- and the loop slips off the fingers.

A single PLUCK.

The hand stays open.

SUPER: "The string slipped. Nobody noticed."
```

```text
South of the Wall: a table with two bowls. North of it: a

No music at all.

SUPER (appears all at once, no typing): "Nobody talks about

Over the north half of the bandstand hangs a crayon banner:
MINGLE.

A pink paint roller slides in from the left -- squeak -- and
leaves a new word behind it: GIGGLE.

The music box comes back, thinner. One voice.

SUPERS pop on, one per beat: "Now there's a different

A beat of silence. The music box takes up its phrase again,
```

### Page 3 of 4

```text
                                                          3.

MAYOR GIGGS (40s), a round cherub with busy little wings, a
top hat on a pompadour, a handlebar moustache and a bow tie,
up a ladder with a paint roller, humming the Mingle.

He stops humming. On the same note.

He blinks.

                      MAYOR GIGGS
          ...Hee. Hee.

Below, the GIGGLING FOUNTAIN's two stone faces giggle
back -- a quarter-second late.

Behind the pink paint, the wet N of MINGLE blooms back
through.

He rolls over it. Squeak. It blooms back.

He rolls again. Squeak, lower. It blooms back bigger.

He rolls again. Squeak, lowest. It blooms back biggest,
red this time, and a red drip runs down.
(Paint: CANDY's deepest red, not blood. The animatic
boards an A/B with the drip left pink. PILOT_ROADMAP.md
D16.)

He whips out a cupcake balloon, ties it over the N --
tink -- and pats it twice.

                      MAYOR GIGGS
          Perfect.

He glances south: at the boarded-up half of the bandstand,
and the Wall beyond it.

He stops. His wings stop.

INSERT - (CRAYON) THE SMALL EMPTY HAND

Four frames. The loop of string sliding away. PLUCK.

BACK TO SCENE

Everything exactly as it was -- except the cupcake balloon
has turned to face the Wall.

A small rubber creak.

The Mayor's eyes slide to it. He reaches up and turns it
back to face the town. Squeak.

                      MAYOR GIGGS
                (a firm pat)
          Stay.
```

```text
                                                          4.

He climbs down, briskly. Clack, clack, clack. The balloon
stays put.

For now.

EXT. FOUNTAIN PLAZA - DAWN

High and wide: HaHaland waking up pastel. Citizens string
lanterns between the lamp posts, a ripple of bunnies, bears
and frogs. A poster on a lamp post: MIDSUMMER GIGGLE
TONIGHT! (half the park, all the fun!)

At the far edge of the plaza, a figure in a giant bunny
head -- MASCOTTY -- stands perfectly still in a crowd that
never stops moving.

We drift across the plaza toward the bakery.

EXT. NANA'S BAKERY - WINDOW - DAWN

Two of everything in the window: two loaves, two buns, two
cakes.

A woolly paw -- NANA PUDDIN's -- tucks a tea towel round an
EGG in a basket and sets a small lamp beside it. The paw
stays a moment. Then it goes.

A tag on the basket turns in the warm air: "for whoever's
next."

CLOSE ON THE EGG

Far off, a bus horn: two notes.

The egg wobbles.

The horn again, nearer: three notes.

The egg wobbles harder.

A heart iris closes on it, and holds, in silence --

CRACK.

                                               SMASH CUT TO:

MAIN TITLE - "DOOMTASTIC TIMES"


                      END OF COLD OPEN
```
