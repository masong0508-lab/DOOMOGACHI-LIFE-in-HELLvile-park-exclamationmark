### The pilot in a paragraph

Eleven years after HaHaland closed half of Hellvile Park, the town throws the Midsummer Giggle on the old Mingle night: half a festival, in half a park, with no fireworks. Sunny, a silent bunnykin newcomer, steps off a pink bus into it, gets a muffin with a horned key in it and an egg from Nana Puddin, and helps Mayor Giggs find the three pages of a one-sentence speech while he paints over every trace of the old Mingle. At nine o'clock, the hour the fireworks used to go up, fog pours over the Wall and Bonejangles, the skeleton bandleader, is possessed by a rhyming breath that rates everything out of ten. The egg hatches in the stampede ("beep."). Father Fuzz runs his first rite in eleven years and loses two of his three candles, while Sunny and the hour-old pet fight through the fair, first through HaHaland's cute filter and then, with a record scratch, as it really is (DOOM-red, in first person), to fetch Nana's recipe tin. Inside is the sign-up sheet from the last Mingle, and the name the rite needs is written on it, but nobody on this side will say it, until Pip, crying, shouts "CLINKER!" The breath goes home through the gate's keyhole, a voice on the far side answers on time ("...Seven."), and the Mayor lets the patrol have one firework. It opens over the Wall as a pink heart, and a small purple one answers from the south. After the credits, something behind the gate eats Sunny's "1" and purrs.

### The main title song: "Doomtastic Times"

The revised, slightly J-pop theme is the show's opening, produced in parallel for [theme/](theme/). It is used five ways in the pilot.

1. **The main title (the 0:50 cut, at 01:40).** The cold open ends on the egg cracking, and the crack is frame 0 of the main title and the song's first downbeat. It is boarded by song section, so it conforms to the master when it lands:

   | Section | Length | Picture |
   |---|---|---|
   | Intro | 0:06 | Crane up over pastel HaHaland; the logo letters drop on the intro hits |
   | A-melo (verse) | 0:13 | The cast pops in on the beat. Mascotty never pops in: she is simply in every shot, perfectly still |
   | B-melo (pre-chorus) | 0:07 | **The mirrored knock.** A brick wipe splits the frame down the Wall; both sides run at it and knock on the same beat. The Hellvile side is silhouettes only |
   | Drop | 0:02 | The song's drop is a record scratch onto the gate: seven padlocks, KEEP OUT. FOREVER., and a small barred grille, its shutter shut (the plant for 102) |
   | Sabi (chorus) | 0:13 | Hellify rots the town; the first-person corridor flips from the filter to the truth on the sweetest line (a Puff Demon bursts into red mist across the lens while the vocal keeps smiling); three candles go out in rhythm; behind the gate, a big round rear silhouette presses against the shut grille and bounces once, BOING, and the padlocks jump |
   | End pose | 0:05 | The two halves meet on the logo; the Mayor's pink paint roller crosses it and paints over the red drip (squeak); on the last chord the drip bleeds back through |
   | Pilot title card | 0:04 | "beep." and the sinking purple sparkle; GIGGLE NIGHT, with MINGLE showing through the paint |

   The bus drive at the top of Act One stretches or shrinks by up to 5 s, so a master anywhere from 0:45 to 0:55 still gives a 22:00 episode.

   **The cute theme over a brutal show is part of the joke.** "Doomtastic Times" stays exactly what the theme team is making: bright, sugary, slightly J-pop, with clean, sincere lyrics; nobody rewrites it edgier. The picture does the brutality: the chorus lands the corridor's switch to the truth (blood and gibs across the lens) on its sweetest line while the vocal keeps smiling, and the end pose paints over a red drip that bleeds back through. The Act Three insert song is the same chorus in its DOOM-side arrangement under real gore; the credits are a music box over crayon stills of the night, drawn as HaHaland remembers it, filter on. The theme line ("We don't talk about the other side.") is still never sung. Nothing in the theme package changes.
2. **The insert song.** The 1:30 TV size comes back in Act Three, anime-style: its DOOM-side chorus scores the two first-person runs (70 s in all). If the master has no DOOM-side stem, the game's E-minor riff carries the scene and the hook is quoted on the last blast.
3. **Eyecatch bumpers, "the festival clock".** Three OUT/IN pairs of 5 s each at 07:50, 12:15 and 16:40. The OUT card is pure HaHaland over a 2-bar cut of the hook in major. The IN card opens on a 4-frame hellify flash and returns with exactly one change, over the same hook in minor resolving on the ITS_FINE sting. The clock on the card tells the hour (21:00, 21:40, 22:59). Mascotty stands 8 px tall in every IN card and comes about 9.6% closer each episode, filling the frame only in the episode where her head comes off (S3E11). Every card is a seamless loop, usable as a chapter marker or a short.
4. **End credits:** a music-box arrangement of the hook under crayon stills of the night.
5. **A living opening.** One detail changes per arc: the far glow blinks back (from 105), the gate has a crack (Season 2), pipes show under the town (after the midpoint), and in the finale the pan has no Wall. The pilot variant keeps Grumbleguts out of the verse; from 102 he pops in rear first with a record scratch, the only cast member who enters backwards, in a verse shot of his own with no child in it.

The master is needed by **W17 (indie) or W24 (solo)**. The package to ask the theme team for in W2 is listed in `PRODUCTION_ROADMAP.md` §4.6: both cuts with a bar map, a DOOM-side chorus stem, 2-bar hook stems in major and minor, a music-box hook, instrumentals and stems, timed lyrics, a split sheet and the licence.
