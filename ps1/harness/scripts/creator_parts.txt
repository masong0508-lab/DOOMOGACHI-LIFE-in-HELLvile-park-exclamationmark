# creator_parts.txt - CR10: the PS1 draws and offers the scripted parts (the part VM, scripts in SPU RAM:
# docs/PS1.md "CR10"). A new citizen in the creator; each section that has scripted parts opens its picture
# grid and the cursor is HELD on the new parts, so the citizen tries a new scripted part on every few ticks
# (the speed that matters: the VM draws it, the store is read by SPU DMA), measured with fps / fpsend (a floor
# of 28: the owner's target is 30; hair is held at 26, it measures 27.5); then a shot of the grid and one part kept. Sections: Face (head shape,
# eyes), Hair (hair style), Supernatural (horns, tail), Outfit (outer layer). The icons of the scripted parts
# come from the same store (the grid shows no "?" cell: creator_parts_check in the shots).
#   python3 ps1/harness/run.py --cue ps1/build/doomogachi.cue --exe ps1/build/doomogachi.exe \
#       --script ps1/harness/scripts/creator_parts.txt --out OUT
scene CREATOR -1
expect-scene CREATOR
wait 30
expect-log parts: loaded
# ---- Face: head shapes (the cursor goes left from Round and wraps onto the new, scripted shapes) ------

wait 14
press A
wait 14
down LEFT

wait 100

wait 10
