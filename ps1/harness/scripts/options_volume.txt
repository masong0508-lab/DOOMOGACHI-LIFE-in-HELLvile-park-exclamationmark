# Options volume (the owner's report: turning the volume down froze the game while the
# memory card was read and written, once per step). Music volume down 5 and up 5 on the Quick
# page: no frame may take over 50 ms (3 vsyncs) while it changes; the settings file is written
# once, when B leaves the Options (expect-count).
scene TITLE
wait 60
push OPTIONS 0
wait 30
press DOWN
wait 10
log optvol: changing
fps optvol
press LEFT
wait 6
press LEFT
wait 6
press LEFT
wait 6
press LEFT
wait 6
press LEFT
wait 6
press RIGHT
wait 6
press RIGHT
wait 6
press RIGHT
wait 6
press RIGHT
wait 6
press RIGHT
wait 6
wait 30
fpsend optvol
expect-gap 50 optvol
log optvol: leaving
fps optexit
press B
wait 90
fpsend optexit
expect-scene TITLE
# a second visit: the file exists now, so this is an in-place rewrite; a visit
# that changes nothing writes nothing
push OPTIONS 0
wait 30
press DOWN
press LEFT
wait 10
fps optexit2
press B
wait 90
fpsend optexit2
push OPTIONS 0
wait 30
press DOWN
press UP
press B
wait 30
expect-scene TITLE
log optvol: done
expect-count 2 memcard: config write
