## Motion pass (Oct 25, the creator's no-slideshow bar; `# motion:` lines in a1.shw)

Lint: `cd show/tools && python3 -m showtk.lint.anim_density plan ep101 --file a1` (or `show lint ep101`).
Before: 53 of 55 shots warned (ANIM-CAM 48, ANIM-GESTURE 26, ANIM-DENSITY 11, ANIM-LISTEN 10). After: **0 warnings**. Shots touched: 51 of 55. Frame total (9,600 f), shot lengths and every line id are unchanged; no new compiler errors.

What was added (each line carries a `# motion:` tag, so the next pass can find or strip it):
- **Camera direction** on every 2D shot of 45 f or more that had no move. Every push is a `cam to scale=` paired with the `cam pan` that keeps the frame's centre still, because the stage camera scales about 0,0; the small extra drift stays inside the plate's margin. The camera type follows the shot: a slow push-in (1.08) on a bust dialogue close-up, a 1.05 push drifting toward the speaker on a two-shot or wider dialogue shot, a 1.06 track toward the mover on action, a 1.06 crane-in reveal on wides and crowds, and a 1.10 ease-OUT push on inserts and cards. Each push is built on the shot's own setup scale (frame-target setups included).
- **A gesture on every line**: `take beat_gesture` (ART's `<char>_pop_small`) for figure and chibi cast with cycles, a nod pair (`headturn 16`/`240`) for busts, and a `TWT_HOP1` plush hop for the rest. Lines spoken inside a deadpan, freeze or still, and actors authored `idle=still` (possessed Bonejangles, Mascotty), are left alone: the stillness is the gag.
- **Listeners react**: a glance toward the speaker on the first line (`headturn 24`), a blink on alternate lines, a `take react_small` (or a hop) on the last line, and the head back 8 f later.
- **Impacts**: SHK_TAP on every impact SFX (THUD/SLAM/BONK/DROP/...) that had no shake within 3 f; SHK_SHOTGUN camera kick on every FP BLAM.
- **Walk cycles**: a raw `walk` of a cycle character becomes `take walk_to` (cycle, then idle), or `pose <a> walk|run` when an authored pose lands at the walk's end. Over 1.8 px/f is a run; an off-screen target is an exit. Actors holding a prop pose keep it (no cycle yet).
- **Takes**: an `expr EXPR_SHOCK` on a cycle character gains `pose <a> take_big` (anticipation, stretch, overshoot, settle).
- Hand fixes: a1_09a's 30-f insert pushes onto the tick (1.5 to 1.58). a1_10c's slow pan was a setup `cam pan`, which the compiler drops, so it is timed now (`+0 cam pan dx=1`). The three `member mascotty` crowd lines (a1_07b, a1_11a, a1_13a) did not parse, which dropped the whole act from the compile. They are now a still `actor mascotty ... idle=still` beside the crowd (TODO lane 2: crowd `member`).
- 4-f whiplash inserts (a1_09e, a1_13c, a1_14d) are flash cuts, so they cannot hold for 12 f; the lint exempts shots of 12 f or less.
- 1-14 is a protected beat: no whips or extra business there (the a1_14b to a1_14c whip candidate was left out).
