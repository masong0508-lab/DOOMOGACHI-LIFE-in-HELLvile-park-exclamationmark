# core/data/home/blueprints.txt - the 12 ROM blueprints (owner: H1; HF sec.2.3):
# one per theme, "Nana's Starter" and Ch3's "Shelter". furngen (H4) joins
# them into core/gen/furn_data.c (doomo_home_blueprints[]).
#
# Applying one puts the room's own pieces back in storage, paints the walls
# in the blueprint's theme and places every piece you own; the rest stay as
# grey ghosts with a "Buy at HomeMart" hint, so a blueprint doubles as a
# shopping list. Two more slots are saved in HOME (the player's own).
#
#   [bp key] ROOM "Name (<= 20)"      ROOM: LIVING BEDROOM KITCHEN CELLAR
#                                     GARDEN ANNEX TOWERS_7B PITS_FLAT PARK_PLOT
#     theme THEME_ID                  (optional) the walls and floor
#     piece KEY X Y F                 a catalog.txt key at tile (X, Y), facing
#                                     F: S (+y), E (+x), N, W. Wall pieces: S on
#                                     wall A (Y = 0), E on wall B (X = 0)
#     small KEY X Y F SUB             a small on the surface at (X, Y), at
#                                     half-tile SUB 0..3 (bit 0 +x, bit 1 +y)
#     paints A B                      (optional, after a piece) PAINT_* or DEFAULT
#
# tests: every ROM blueprint applies in its own room with nothing blocked.
