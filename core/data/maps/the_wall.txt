# The Wall - Hell Breach 12 (owner: LEVELS-3; plan section 3, HB12). A DARK LEVEL: content note, ambient 3.
# The Wall is hollow: the labourers' bunks and chalk. 64 x 20, a long run west from the gatehouse with three
# stacked galleries (0, 12, 24; lifts) to the tally room and the Foreman's yard under the sky. THE TWO FACES:
# one swap set `faceb` (event 30, a lever: every `l`/`m` pad on a trigger cell flips it). State A (start):

# State B: the opposite. So the way west is: lift 1, flip (B), lift 2, flip (A) past the barricade, flip (B)
# at the pump gallery, the tally room, the yard. `face_a` / `face_b` zones mark which actors are real on which
# face. PS1 and Dreamcast only (`gba no`). Lever pads are trigger cells for now (LV17: A at a lever posts tag 30).
