# Graveyard Shift - Hell Breach 5 (owner: levels expansion).
# Night shift at the HaHaland cemetery. Somebody is crying in the garden.
#   The cemetery gate under the stars (picket fence, rows of tombstones, an
#   open grave you can climb into - secret) -> two terraces up the hill
#   (stairs cut into the slope, the sealed mausoleum in the hill face) ->
#   the hilltop chapel (pews, stained glass, an altar) -> the trapdoor lift
#   behind the altar drops into the crypts inside the hill (catacombs with
#   skull niches, a flooded crypt with stepping stones, a crawlspace to a
#   hidden tomb - secret) -> the ossuary (red key) -> the lift up into the
#   mausoleum, whose door opens from the inside -> the red garden gate ->
#   the Widow's sunken garden (hedges, a moonlit pond, gargoyle pedestals)
#   and the mini-boss THE WEEPING WIDOW (a huge, ghostly vape wraith that
#   blinks between the graves). Her death unlocks the exit gate.
# Built with the levels team's map kit; edit freely. Compiled by tools/mapc.py.
