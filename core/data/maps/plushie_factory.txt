# Plushie Factory - Hell Breach 4 (owner: levels expansion).
# Where HaHaland's plushies are made... and where the demons sew in new eyes.
#   The loading yard under a sunset sky (crate stairs up to a secret shed
#   roof) -> the Assembly Hall: three conveyor belts (cells tagged 240..243
#   push you east/south/west/north - the levels runtime moves you along),
#   a catwalk overhead, a secret door in a machine -> the Stuffing Vats:
#   fluffy stuffing pits you can fall into (a stuffing pile lets you climb
#   out), vats of boiling glue (lava), a machine lift to a secret ledge, the
#   blue key on the press -> the Sewing Room, where plushies come alive
#   (ambush) and the walls grow eyes -> the blue door -> the Furnace: a lava
#   channel under an open smokestack, and FOREMAN FLUFFNUTS (a giant cyber
#   piggy) in his office. His death opens the shipping chute (exit).
# Built with the levels team's map kit; edit freely. Compiled by tools/mapc.py.
