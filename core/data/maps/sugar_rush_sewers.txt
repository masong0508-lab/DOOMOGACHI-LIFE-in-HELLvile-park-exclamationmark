# Sugar Rush Sewers - Hell Breach 3 (owner: levels expansion).
# Under Gumdrop Park the storm drains run with strawberry soda... at first.
#   Gumdrop Park (open sky, hedges, a planter-stair secret garden) -> the
#   gatehouse stairs down into the Candy Canal (soda channel, grate bridges,
#   a low candy beam, a secret pantry behind a striped wall) -> the walls
#   turn to flesh and the soda to slime -> the Sluice House balcony (red
#   key) -> the sluice lift down into the Gut Sewer (slime channel) -> the
#   red door -> the Cistern: a deep shaft open to the pink sky, a sludge
#   pool with an island, a lift to a hidden ledge, and the mini-boss
#   GUMDROP GUTTERKING (a giant slime belcher). Its death opens the exit
#   stairs back up to a cute manhole.
# Built with the levels team's map kit; edit freely. Compiled by tools/mapc.py.
