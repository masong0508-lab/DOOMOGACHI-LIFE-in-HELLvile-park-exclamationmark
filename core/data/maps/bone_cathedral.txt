# Bone Cathedral - Hell Breach 6 (owner: levels expansion).
# A gothic hell church that goes UP. Mind the organ.
#   The courtyard under the red sky (bone pillars, the lava font, a hidden
#   nook behind the east pillars - secret) -> the 40-step-high facade and
#   its great arch -> the narthex with two spiral stair towers (11 steps around
#   a pillar each) -> the nave: pews, a carpet aisle and a 320-unit vault;
#   its crossing is open to the sky over a lava pool -> the galleries along
#   both sides of the nave (96 units up) -> the west transept and the bell
#   tower lift (blue key in the bell chamber, a ledge above it - secret) ->
#   the east transept's bone chapel (a secret door behind the altar) ->
#   drop back into the nave -> the stairs to the blue door -> the apse,
#   open to the sky, where a wall of organ pipes up to 280 units tall
#   plays for BISHOP BONEBOTTOM, THE ORGAN GRINDER (a giant bone jester;
#   the pipes shoot fire while he lives). His death opens the centre pipes
#   and the way out.
# Built with the levels team's map kit; edit freely. Compiled by tools/mapc.py.
