# core/data/parts/face/a1a_eyes.txt - CR4 batch A1a: 24 eye styles (values 24..47 of eye_style).
# Frame blocks (anchor=frame): one block per eye, 256 = the eye radius, +x is outward on both eyes
# (the left eye is mirrored), y down. The iris colour is the citizen's eye colour; the dark tone of
# the same ramp is the ink. Blinks, sleepy, happy, love and dead faces are drawn by the painter.

def glint X Y
  mat white
  decal
  dot $X $Y 2
enddef

list eye_style 24                   # Bean: a tall narrow oval
block group=3 anchor=frame lod=all view=all
  mat eye_color
  shade vgrad
  solid noline
  ellipse 0 0 164 254
  use glint -50 -110
end
