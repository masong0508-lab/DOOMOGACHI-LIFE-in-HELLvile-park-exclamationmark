# (The v1 save stream gives the aura 4 bits; values 16..23 ride in the creator trailer, look_gene.c.)
# Hand-authored. An aura is drawn behind the citizen (group 0) and in front of it (group 6), head anchor,
# `scale 128` (half head radii: x 128 = the head's side, y -128 = the top), `solid noline,nocast` so a
# particle has no outline. The animation is two frames, `ifanim breath` against its absence.
# Auras draw on the bust, the figure and the fighter, never on a chibi (crowds pay nothing).

  shade flat
  solid noline,nocast
