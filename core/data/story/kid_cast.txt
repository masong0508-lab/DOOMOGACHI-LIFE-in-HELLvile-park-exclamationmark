#   E1's adult gate (mature_adult_ok): nobody here is ever in a mature scene
#
# Format, one entry a line, '#' comments:
#   ID<TAB>Name        a premade citizen; the name must match the roster
#                      (so a renumbered or renamed citizen fails the lint)
#   @E<TAB>the pet     the hero's pet: never in sexual or mature content
#                      (DECISIONS: nudity rule; it is with the hero in most
#                      scenes, so it bars the sexual class and mature
#                      branches, not the LOVE face or swearing)
#   @scene<TAB>GLOB    a kids' scene with no kid citizen in its cast (a
#                      daycare full of unnamed children, a child's crayon
#                      flashback): every script whose name, or whose pack's
#                      name, matches GLOB counts as having a kid in it
#
# Every KID citizen a script names must be listed here (the lint checks
# it), so E1 and LS0 see the whole story's kid cast. Teens are not listed:
# a teen may date a teen (LS0), and the TEEN trait already bars adult
# material from their scenes.
