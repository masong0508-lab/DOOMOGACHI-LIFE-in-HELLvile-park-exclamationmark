# assert-no-stubs
# T9: the pointer on the Talk screen (docs/TALK.md "The pointer"). Every target is reached with "mouse X Y
# click" and does what the matching pad press does; the pad is only used to page the dialogue box.
# A negative X / Y counts from the right / bottom edge, so one script fits 240x160 and 320x240: the panel is
# 90 px tall on the main page, shorter on compact submenus. panel+N is relative to the actual panel top
# (header +5, then rows of 12 px: slot i is at panel+22 plus 12 i). The people
# strip is the top 24 px, the scroll bar is the right edge of the list (X -4).
scene TITLE
wait 10
push HOME 3
wait 4
press B
wait 10
push TALK 1
wait 6
press A
wait 30
press A
wait 30
expect-scene TALK
expect-ptr-page replies
shot talk_ptr_0_replies
# a pointer with no click changes nothing
mouse 100 -40
expect-scene TALK
expect-ptr-page replies
shot talk_ptr_1_hover
# the header on the main page is not "Say goodbye": nothing happens
mouse 100 -85 click
expect-scene TALK
expect-ptr-page replies
# a category row (slot 4, after the four replies) opens its acts; the header goes back (B)
mouse 100 -20 click
expect-ptr-page acts
shot talk_ptr_2_acts
mouse 100 panel+5 click
expect-ptr-page replies
# the scroll bar: lower half = R (a page down: sel 0 -> 6), upper half = L (a page up: back to 0), R again
mouse -10 -20 click
expect-ptr-row "Be cool"
mouse -10 -60 click
mouse -10 -20 click
expect-ptr-row "Be cool"
# keep going down to the last page: the last three rows are always the typed line, Options and goodbye
mouse -10 -20 click
mouse -10 -20 click
mouse -10 -20 click
shot talk_ptr_3_last_page
# Options (slot 4) -> the setup page (Topic, Invite: you are alone with Nana)
mouse 100 -20 click
expect-ptr-page options
shot talk_ptr_4_options
# Topic (slot 0) -> the topic page; the header goes back to the replies
mouse 100 panel+22 click
expect-ptr-page topics
shot talk_ptr_5_topics
mouse 100 panel+5 click
expect-ptr-page replies
# The shared-life, needs and oddity rows lengthened the real Talk menu.
# Four page-down pointer clicks (not the old three) reach the Options row
# after returning from the Topics sheet on both native resolutions.
mouse -10 -20 click
mouse -10 -20 click
mouse -10 -20 click
mouse -10 -20 click
mouse 100 -20 click
expect-ptr-page options
# Invite someone (slot 1) -> who joins in (slot 0 is the one you are closest to); a click joins them in
mouse 100 panel+34 click
expect-ptr-page invite
shot talk_ptr_6_invite
mouse 100 panel+22 click
expect-scene DIALOG
press A
wait 30
press A
wait 30
expect-scene TALK
expect-ptr-page replies 0
# the people strip: the second name, then the first again (to-slot 1, then 0)
mouse -50 10 click
expect-ptr-page replies 1
mouse 10 10 click
expect-ptr-page replies 0
shot talk_ptr_7_people
# the typed line (slot 3 of the last page): the keyboard opens
mouse -10 -20 click
mouse -10 -20 click
mouse -10 -20 click
mouse -10 -20 click
mouse 100 -32 click
expect-scene TEXT_ENTRY
press B
wait 4
expect-scene TALK
# a quick reply (slot 0 of the first page): you say it and the dialogue box opens
mouse -10 -60 click
mouse -10 -60 click
mouse -10 -60 click
mouse -10 -60 click
mouse 100 -68 click
expect-scene DIALOG
mouse off
